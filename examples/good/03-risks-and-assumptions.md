## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Response | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The vendor changes its interface during the build | Low | High | Reduce: fix the interface version in the contract | Head of partnerships |
| Depot staff skip the second restock | Medium | Medium | Mitigate: add the restock to the evening shift checklist | Depot operations lead |
| Lockers sit empty at quiet depots | Medium | Low | Accept: review use after three months | Product manager |
| The data processing terms are not signed in time | Medium | High | Avoid: the build does not start until they are signed | Legal counsel |

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Status | How it was tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers will collect parcels within three days | Tested | Survey of 300 depot customers |
| The vendor test system behaves like production | Tested | Prototype calls compared with the vendor production logs |
| Two restocks a day are enough at the largest depots | Tested | Parcel counts of the last six weeks at both depots |
