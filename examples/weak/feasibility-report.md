# Feasibility study: customer portal rebuild

Verdict: go

## Background

The current portal is slow and the support team gets many complaints about it. A rebuild looks like a good idea. A
proof of concept was built by one developer last month.

Budget: $180,000

## Technical feasibility

The new portal would use the same API as the mobile app. Most of the screens can be copied from the app.

## Operational feasibility

Rating: probably fine

## Schedule

Rating: TBD

The rebuild will take 16 weeks.

## Risks

- The API may not support every portal feature.
- The key developer may leave. Owner: TBD
- Licence prices may rise. Mitigate by agreeing the price for three years. Owner: Finance lead

## Assumptions

- Customers want a new portal (untested)
- The mobile API is stable. Status: open
- Hosting stays at the same price. Status: confirmed by the platform team

## Recommendation

Stop the work on search until the budget is agreed.

Final verdict: maybe later in the quarter
