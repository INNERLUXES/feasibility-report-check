# A weak report

A made-up feasibility study for rebuilding the customer portal of a made-up company, written the way a rushed study
often is. It holds one problem of most kinds: two verdicts that do not agree and one that is not a verdict, two angles
with no section, an angle with no rating, a rating that says nothing, a rating that is not a rating word, risks with no
response and no owner, assumptions that are still open, a budget and a duration given as one number with no cost
section, a proof of concept with no results, and no next steps.
